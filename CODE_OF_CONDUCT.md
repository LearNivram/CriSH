# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Discuss the code, not the
person writing it.

## What is expected

- Welcoming and patient language, especially towards newcomers.
- Accepting review comments as being about the change, not about you.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it.
- Showing empathy when someone gets something wrong; everyone does.

## What is not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, in pull requests, in commit messages and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening an issue, or privately to the maintainers if that
is not appropriate. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject contributions that do not follow this document, and may ban
contributors for behaviour they judge harmful.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
